## The one-line version

We are entering **Track 2 - Education and Student Success**. We are building a product that turns a student's actual university coursework into a personalised, industry-referenced skill roadmap for the roles they want, and then connects them to real local networking events so the roadmap ends in a conversation rather than another certificate.

It runs for the **whole degree, from first semester to graduation**, re-deriving each semester as their units complete and the industry moves. The point is to keep students continuously aligned with the industry they are heading into, so the gap closes incrementally instead of arriving as a crisis in final year. Primary user: any student at an Australian university, at any stage of their course.
